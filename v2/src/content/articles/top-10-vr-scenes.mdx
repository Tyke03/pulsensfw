Heat is the tax. A scene earns it when the first minute has a point of view and the tenth minute still does.

This example ten is a placeholder ranking so the Top 10 row has a VR card. The real list, when the desk is publishing, will name scenes and say why each one survives a long session.

1. A room-scale piece with somewhere to sit.
2. A seated piece that does not punish you for leaning back.
3. The rest, only if the menu is not longer than the scene.
