Danger was one of the most-viewed performers on Pornhub at the peak of the platform era, and she also directed. The profile is not a comeback watch. It is a record of a performer who used the leverage of that run to move into a different life.

That makes the entity record more useful than a ranking. There is no next release to chase. There is a body of work, a directing credit, and a clear date when the performer stopped.

If you are looking for the current version of this career, look at creators who are building direct audiences now. Danger's run is a useful comparison point: what the studio-plus-tube model produced, and what it cost to leave.
