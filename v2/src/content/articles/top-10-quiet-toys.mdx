This is a seed list, not a store. The order is editorial fiction with a real sorting rule: lower noise beats higher power when someone sleeps on the other side of the plaster.

1. Lumen Wand Mini, for the low floor.
2. Anything with a travel lock you trust in a drawer.
3. A pebble that stays quiet and gives up the app.
4. Through 10. The rest of the list is the same idea with worse motors.
