1. Turn off anything that syncs chats to a second device you share.
2. Export once, so you know the door exists.
3. Write a three-line card.
4. Test memory with a boring fact.
5. Test voice with a stopwatch, then decide.
6. Name the boundary out loud in the card, not in a fight.
7. through 10. Delete the features you will not open.

The first week is setup. The personality can wait until the plumbing is honest.
