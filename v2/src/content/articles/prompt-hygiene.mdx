Week two is when a companion starts to sand itself down. The fix is not a longer prompt. It is a smaller one you actually maintain.

Write the name, the boundary, and one concrete preference. Leave the lore for the chat. When the tone drifts, paste the short card back instead of arguing with the drift.

## A card that fits on a phone
Three lines. If it does not fit, you will not reuse it, and the bot will not either.
