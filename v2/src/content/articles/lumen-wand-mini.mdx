Most wands win the spec sheet and lose the apartment. This example one has a slider that starts low, which is rarer than the marketing implies.

Mode four is the one you will not use with a roommate home. The app paired on the first try and did not ask for a social login. Battery matched the claim within ten minutes, which is as close as a seed test gets to a citation.
