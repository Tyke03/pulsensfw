Noise is the spec people lie about. We held the Mini an arm's length from a phone mic on a quiet night and wrote down the number you see in the lab card. It is an example measurement, and it is also the right kind of measurement.

The motor has a low floor. That is rarer than a high ceiling. The ceiling is there too, and it gets buzzy if you camp on the top pattern. Most of the useful range sits underneath that.

## Care
Rinse the head, dry the seam, and do not store it in a tangled cable. The travel lock survived a backpack in this seed test.
