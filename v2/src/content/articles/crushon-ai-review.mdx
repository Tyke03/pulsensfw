CrushOn AI’s pitch is straightforward: unfiltered character roleplay, a huge community-built card library, and a free tier that lets you test the chat loop before paying. The dossier above holds the verified plan numbers, pros, cons, and source links. Here is what matters once you look past the headline pricing.

## The character library

The library is still CrushOn’s strongest draw. If you are looking for a specific trope, fandom card, or scenario setup, someone in the community has usually built a version of it already. The tradeoff is the same one you see on every open card directory: card quality varies wildly, and the most popular cards on the front page are not always the best written.

## Pricing and the free tier

The free tier is usable enough to judge whether you like the interface and response style: you get 100 monthly credits plus unlimited access to designated free models with an 8K memory window. It is strictly a sample, though—free chat history is wiped after seven days of inactivity.

If you look at paid plans, watch the billing toggle carefully. The headline `$4.9/mo` rate on the pricing page is **annual billing** (`$58.88/year`, effectively `$4.91/mo`). Month-to-month Standard is `$5.99/mo`.

## Billing, privacy, and why it dropped off our Top 10

CrushOn AI previously sat inside our Top 10, but a stack of trust and billing red flags pushed it out of our [2026 AI companion rankings](/story/best-ai-companion-apps-2026):

- **Cancellation friction & refunds:** Community reports flag the lack of straightforward self-serve cancellation/upgrades (forcing users to let a term lapse) alongside non-refundable current periods and mid-flight payment processor changes.
- **Privacy posture:** Third-party privacy reviews have flagged heavy tracker counts (~45 trackers reported), chat-log collection, and no confirmed end-to-end encryption.
- **App distribution ambiguity:** The official FAQ references mobile apps, yet verified App Store / Google Play listings could not be confirmed at check time.

Because of our editorial policy, **we have paused CrushOn AI’s affiliate promotion** until these billing and cancellation concerns are resolved.

## Bottom line

If you want to browse community cards on the free tier using a burner email, CrushOn AI works as a quick testbed. If you are thinking about paying for a monthly subscription, compare cleaner alternatives first—including [SpicyChat](/ai/spicychat), [Janitor AI](/ai/janitor-ai), or our [4-way side-by-side comparison](/compare?ids=crushon-ai,janitor-ai,spicychat,kindroid). If you do pay, use a virtual/disposable card.
