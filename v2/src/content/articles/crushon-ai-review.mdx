CrushOn's pitch is simple: a huge character library, persistent memory, and a free tier that lets you decide before you subscribe. The record below has the plans and the sources. This review is about the parts a price table cannot tell you.

The library is the product. If you want a specific archetype, the odds are good that someone has already made a card for it. The catch is the same as every community library: quality varies, and the best cards are not always the most visible.

The free tier is honest enough to test the core loop, but it is not a way to use the app for free indefinitely: 100 monthly credits plus unlimited access to the designated free models, and free chat history is deleted after seven days of inactivity. Treat it as a sample. One billing trap: the $4.9/mo headline on the pricing page is annual billing — month-to-month is $5.99.
