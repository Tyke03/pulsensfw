Payout week is when a platform's personality shows up as a number. In this example cycle the fee did not move. The delay did. Creators who used to see funds on Wednesday saw them on Friday, with no note in the dashboard.

That is the whole story, and it is enough. A silent slip is worse than a posted fee, because you cannot plan around a change you have to infer.

## What to watch
Whether Friday becomes the new normal, or whether this was a one-week clog.
