Sit down before you put it on. The hero shot of someone standing in the middle of the room is how people hit a shelf.

Tighten the top strap until the gasket touches all the way around, then back off a finger. If the lenses fog, the room is warm or the face pad is sealed too hard. Point a fan at your chest, not at the lenses.

Volume goes in headphones. The speakers in the strap are for the trailer.

This is example copy on the phase 1 desk, not a published review.
