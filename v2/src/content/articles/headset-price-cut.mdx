An example price cut, not a coupon. Entry headsets get interesting when the number drops under the cost of a regretted impulse and the return window is still boringly normal.

The fit did not change because the price did. If the stock strap printed a line into your forehead last year, it still will. Budget for the strap, or do not budget for the headset.

## Who should wait
Anyone who needs glasses-friendly fit and has not tried a demo. A cheaper regret is still a regret.
