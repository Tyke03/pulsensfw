Pornhub is the default because it is the easiest. Search works, the player is fast, and the library is large enough that most searches return something. That is the whole case for the free tier.

The cost is attention. The free tier is an ad product, and the front page is optimized for the studio catalog rather than the thing you came for. Premium removes most of that friction, but the price is shown at checkout and varies by region, so this page does not pretend to know your number.

Privacy is the other cost. The site is mainstream enough that payment and account handling are familiar, but "mainstream" is not the same as "private." If discretion matters, use the reader controls on this site and think about what you are logged into.

For a free tube, it is still the reference point. For a curated library or a specific niche, a premium site is usually the better spend.
