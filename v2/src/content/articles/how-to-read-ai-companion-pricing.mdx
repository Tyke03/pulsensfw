Most AI companion pricing pages are designed to answer one question: what is the cheapest number I can put on this button? The number is real. It is also rarely the number you will pay after the first month.

## Start with memory, not messages

A plan that gives you more messages but the same context window is a trap for long-form roleplay. The character forgets the scene and you spend the evening re-establishing it. Look for the context number and the memory features before you look at the message cap.

## Separate chat from media

Images, voice, and video usually run on a second meter: tokens, coins, or credits. A subscription can include a small monthly allowance and still leave you buying more. If you want images, price the token pack before you price the subscription.

## Check the cancel path

Find the cancellation page before you pay. If you cannot find it in under a minute, that is information. Also check whether the plan renews at the promotional price or the list price.

## Use the free tier as a test, not a home

Free tiers are for finding out whether the character quality is good enough. They are not a long-term plan. The apps that keep a useful free tier are the exception, and the entity records below mark which ones still do.

## Read the record, then decide

The panels below carry the plans and the sources. Do not trust a single blog post, including this one, for a price that changes by region and app store. Open the checkout, read the renewal line, and take a screenshot.
