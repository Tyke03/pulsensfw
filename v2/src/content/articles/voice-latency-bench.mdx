We tapped a stopwatch when the sentence ended and stopped it when audio started. The median was fine. The worst gap was the one you feel.

Barge-in, talking over the reply, worked sometimes and swallowed the rest. Until that is reliable, voice is a feature you turn on for a mood, not the way you actually talk.
