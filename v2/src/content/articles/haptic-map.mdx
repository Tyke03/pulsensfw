A haptic map is a claim about geography. This one got the geography right often enough that the misses felt like misses, which is a compliment.

The delay is a short breath. In a slow scene you forgive it. In a fast one you feel the caption arrive after the picture. Comfort is fine until the lining warms up.

## Who it is for
People who already like the headset and want the body in the scene, and who will not pretend the lag is not there.
