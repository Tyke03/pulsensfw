SpicyChat is the app you open when you do not yet know what you want. The library is enormous, the roleplay tooling is mature, and the free tier is generous enough to browse before you pay.

The weakness is the pricing language. The tier names do not tell you which upgrade actually fixes the thing you are complaining about. The cheapest paid tier is not the memory upgrade; the record below makes that clear without repeating the whole ladder here.

The other weakness is that the official checkout wants a sign-in before it shows you the full price. That is not unique to SpicyChat, but it is a reason to treat third-party price lists as snapshots rather than promises.

If you want variety, start here. If you want a single character with deep continuity, CrushOn or Kindroid is the better fit.
