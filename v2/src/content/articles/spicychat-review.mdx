SpicyChat is the app you open when you do not yet know what you want. The library is enormous, the roleplay tooling is mature, and the free tier is generous enough to browse before you pay.

The weakness is the pricing language. The tier names do not tell you which upgrade actually fixes the thing you are complaining about. The $5.95 "Get a Taste" tier is verified on the subscription page; the higher tiers carry the memory and TTS upgrades without published prices we could verify, so check the checkout before assuming.

The other sign-in friction is the app itself: there is no currently supported native iOS app, and Android installs as a sideloaded APK rather than a store listing. That is fine for power users and a blocker for casual ones.

If you want variety, start here. If you want a single character with deep continuity, CrushOn or Kindroid is the better fit.
