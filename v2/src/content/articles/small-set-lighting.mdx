You do not need a studio. You need the lamp off the face and a wall that is not busy. Put the key high and to the side. Bounce the other side with a white pillowcase if that is what you have.

The explicit plate on this guide is the set, not a diagram. In SFW mode you get the withheld card instead of the still.

## The test
Film ten seconds, watch it on a phone in another room. If the laundry is the subject, move the lamp, not the speech.
