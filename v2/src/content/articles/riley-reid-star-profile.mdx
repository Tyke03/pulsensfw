The studio years are the part most people know. The direct-to-viewer years are the part that changed the business model. Riley Reid joined OnlyFans in 2020 and later turned the audience into a distribution channel rather than a dependency on a single studio.

The AI companion project is the more unusual move. It puts a performer-owned identity inside a product category that usually treats characters as disposable. Whether that works is an open question; the record below keeps the dates and the platforms so you can judge it without a press release.

For viewers, the practical advice is simple: the catalog is large, the platform mix is wider than it looks, and the name is attached to products beyond the films. Check the record before you assume a page is official.
