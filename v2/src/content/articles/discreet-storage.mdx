The guest test is visual. If a case has to be explained, it is the wrong case. Plain, rigid, not clear, and not under a pile of cables you will dump out while looking for a charger.

The browser test is the other half. Discreet mode on this site swaps the tab title and the icon. Shift+H also blurs the page. Storage cannot help you if the laptop is open on the story.

## Charge where you store
A dead toy in a tasteful box is just a box. Keep the cable in the case.
