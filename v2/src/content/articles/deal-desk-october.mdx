None of the prices on this site are live, and none of them are affiliate links. The deal desk in this shell exists so the row has a shape.

A drop worth flagging is a real previous price, a return window, and a product we have already said something about. A drop that fails is a number that was never the price, printed with a line through it.

## October, as an example
Five cards. All of them say example on the face. When the desk is real, the sentence gets a date and a link that goes to a merchant, with the disclosure on it.
