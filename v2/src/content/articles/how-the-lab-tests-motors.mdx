Phase 1 scores are examples. The method is the part we want to keep.

Noise gets a number at a set distance, not a word like whisper. Battery gets a session with the pattern people actually use, not the dimmest light. Motors get a note on whether the low end exists.

## What a score is not
It is not a moral grade and it is not a medical claim. It is a way to compare one product's annoying traits with another's.

When the lab is real, this page gets the protocol and the scores get dates.
