The test was petty on purpose. A coffee order, a nickname, a plan for Tuesday. Two days later we asked what Tuesday was for.

Two apps invented a confident answer. One app, Northglass in this seed set, repeated the plan and admitted the parts it did not store. That is the behavior worth paying for.

## Privacy, briefly
An export you can read beats a memory you cannot see. The file was messy. It was also there.
