Clear a standing oval. Put a chair just outside it, not inside it. If you are tethered, tape the cable so the loop is boring.

Start seated. The first night is for the interface, not for a scene that asks you to lunge. Glasses users should solve the fit before they solve the library.

## Stop rule
If the brow hurts, you are done, even if the scene is good. Fit problems do not get braver.
