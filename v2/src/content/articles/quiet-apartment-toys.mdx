A wand is a speaker with a hobby. If the walls are thin, start with something that does not have a head the size of a microphone.

Quiet Marble, our example bullet, stays under a podcast. The Lumen's first two modes do too. Mode four does not, and pretending otherwise is how reviews get people in trouble with roommates.

This is example copy on the phase 1 desk, not a published review.
