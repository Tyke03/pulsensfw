The adult career was short. The public life after it was not. Khalifa moved through webcam work, OnlyFans, sports commentary, and a jewelry line, and she has been outspoken about sex workers and about the way platforms treat people who leave.

That makes her a strange fit for a conventional star profile. There is no long filmography to rank. The record is about media, not scenes.

For viewers, the practical note is that the name is attached to a lot of unofficial pages and a lot of old clips. The entity record keeps the verified facts and the public platforms in one place, which is more useful than another "where is she now" post.
