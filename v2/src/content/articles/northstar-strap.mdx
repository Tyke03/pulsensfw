We wore the stock halo and the Northstar on back-to-back nights. The brow was the win. The crown was the invoice.

Stability during a lean was as good as the stock piece. The pad should be washed, and it should not be the only pad you own if you run hot.

## Buy it if
The stock strap is the reason you cut sessions short. Do not buy it hoping to erase every trace. It moves the trace.
