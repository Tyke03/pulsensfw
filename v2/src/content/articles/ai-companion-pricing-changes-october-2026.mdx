Three changes in the last quarter matter more than any new character model.

SpicyChat repriced its plans in September. The important part is not the headline number; it is which tier finally gets the larger context window. The entity record has the ladder.

Kindroid closed its free tier for new Lite users in October. Existing users did not get the same product, and the paid tier is now the only way to use the app as intended. That is a cleaner deal than a permanent free tier that quietly shrinks, but it is still a change worth knowing before you recommend the app to someone.

Janitor AI expanded its paid option without closing the free in-house model. That keeps it in the "try first" category, even though the high-end experience still depends on technical setup.

The pattern is the same across the category: free tiers are becoming trials, and the real comparison is between paid tiers. The records below are the source of truth. If a number changes, update the record, not the article.
