The lab block is simple. Ninety minutes, lights low, a scene that asks the headset to stay put while you forget it is there. The After-Midnight Visor does that better than the stock halo it is standing in for.

Heat still shows up, just later. Around minute seventy the brow pad warms, and the top strap leaves a crease. Tracking did not wander when we stepped out for water, which is the unglamorous test that matters.

## What we would change
A softer top strap, and a battery note that assumes haptics are on. The rest of the fit is the reason this leads the example desk.
