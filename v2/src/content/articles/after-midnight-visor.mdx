The pitch is simple. Put the headset on, walk into a bar that does not exist, and stay for a scene instead of a trailer.

We did that for a week. The picture holds up in the dark, which is the only lighting this genre cares about. The strap is fine for half an hour and bossy after that. Fog on the lenses showed up when the room was warm, not when the scene got loud.

This is example copy on the phase 1 desk, not a published review. The visor, the studio, and the score are seed data so the review layout has something honest to wear.
