The AI companion category has a pricing problem. Almost every app advertises a free tier, almost every free tier is a preview, and the checkout page is often behind a login. This list is ordered by what a paying adult actually gets after the free trial ends: content freedom, memory, character variety, and whether the price survives a second month.

Two links on this page are tracked: CrushOn and SpicyChat. Both are marked in the entity record and both were ranked on the same criteria as the rest. Everything else is a plain link. The price tables live in the records below so this page does not become a second, stale copy of the facts.

## How the ranking works

The top tier is for apps that let you build a recurring character and keep the thread. The middle tier is for breadth or a specific feature. The bottom tier is still useful, but the trade-off is clearer: restricted content, a token meter, or a free tier that no longer exists.

A high score is not a safety badge. Read the privacy and data-breach facts in the record before you enter payment details, especially on apps that are not distributed through the major app stores.

## What to skip

If you only want a free roleplay session, start with the free tiers and do not subscribe on the first night. If you want explicit long-form chat with a consistent character, pay for memory rather than message volume. If you want images, budget for tokens separately; the subscription is rarely the whole bill.
