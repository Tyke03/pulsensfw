The AI companion category has a pricing problem. Almost every app advertises a free tier, almost every free tier is a preview, and the checkout page is often behind a login. This list is ordered by what a paying adult actually gets after the free trial ends: content freedom, memory, character variety, and whether the price survives a second month.

Two links on this runner-up crop are tracked elsewhere on the site; ranking never reads affiliate status. The price tables live in the entity records so this page does not become a second, stale copy of the facts.

## Status: v0.2, under review — now citing both datasets

This revision takes in two files the desk received this morning:

- **The fan-support census** — a community compilation with dated Reddit/Hacker News quotes per platform, each tagged with a mention strength (heavy / moderate / light). It is community evidence, which is allowed for ranking — unlike aggregator SEO lists, every claim here is tied to a dated thread we can link.
- **The scam-trust compilation** — per-platform red flags from 2023–2026 coverage (Mozilla profiles, Trustpilot patterns, reported billing traps, security incidents), each with severity and a verdict (clean / caution / avoid).

The rule applied: **rank only platforms with heavy or moderate fan support and no dealbreaker.** "Avoid" verdicts drop; unranked-in-both is watchlist; every number we could not verify first-party stays null and says so. The ranking criteria remain: memory/long-context quality, NSFW freedom, testability before paying, price transparency and value, community reputation.

## The ranking

1. **SillyTavern** — *heavy fan support; no dealbreaker.* Free, open, zero censorship, any backend. Ranked first on NSFW freedom and testability (nominally you pay nothing but your LLM). Costs: it's a power tool — r/SillyTavernAI's own users call the learning curve steep and heavy agentic use expensive ($150+/mo cited by one user with tool use).
2. **Janitor AI** — *heavy fan support; caution, no billing dealbreaker.* The big free catalog and uncensored text; ranked 2 on community breadth and free entry. The scam-hunt caution stack is acknowledged in the record: a community-reported memory-wipe bug is a practical dealbreaker for memory-heavy use until it's fixed — ranked here, but back up your bots before you pay.
3. **Kindroid** — *heavy fan support; caution, product praised.* Best-in-class docs, real multimedia. But: the 2025 filter pivot changed the deal from unfiltered, store billing costs more than web (~$50/yr gap reported; web unverified), and the Nov 2025 Play removal disrupted renewals. Ranked 3 on feature depth with those warnings attached.
4. **Nomi** — *heavy fan support; caution.* The most honest pricing in the category (one flat plan, no lifetime bait), indefinite free tier. The red flag is operational: community-reported updates have erased trained personalities. Ranked 4 — pay monthly, not annual, until that resolves.
5. **Chub AI** — *heavy fan support; caution.* The giant card library plus BYO-API flexibility. The caution stack is the 2026 restructure: free tier cut (reported ~20/day, unverified) and crypto-only billing — a service that locked card users out. Ranked 5 for heavy-user flexibility, flagged for beginners.
6. **SpicyChat** — *moderate fan support; caution.* Huge library, documented tier table, $5.95 verified first-party. Caution: community-reported support/refund failures and the clone-app trap (no official store app). Ranked 6 — below the heavies, above everything in the avoid tier.
7. **Dreamjourney AI** — *moderate fan support; no scam record.* Memory Nexus automates the one thing every rival makes you do by hand. Ranked 7 on memory design; credit metering and UI complaints keep it out of the top five until pricing is verified first-party.
8. **FictionLab** — *moderate fan support; re-check flagged.* Scenario/world-building platform with in-house 70B+ models. Ranked 8 provisionally — the community praise is dated and consistent, but the site sat behind a Cloudflare challenge at verification, so it carries a re-check flag.
9. **WyvernChat** — *moderate fan support; no scam record.* Unfiltered, unlogged, free rotating models via Featherless, scriptable bots. Ranked 9 on free-access merit; complexity complaints and the absence of a dedicated app cap its position.
10. **DreamGen** — *the scam-hunt "cleanest" verdict in the category.* No red flags of substance: bootstrapped, refunds honored, no security record. It sits 10th only because the fan-support census does not rank it yet — trust merits, not popularity, put it on the board.

## Dropped since v0.1 (named, not hidden)

- **CrushOn AI** — formerly 5th. The scam compilation's verdict is **avoid**: reported no manual cancel (let the sub lapse and re-subscribe), non-refundable terms, payment-processor churn, ~45 trackers with no confirmed encryption, and an operator identity that doesn't line up. That is a dealbreaker stack. The entity record stays — with a disposable-card warning — and the tracked link remains gated, but it does not rank.
- **CHAI** — formerly 9th. Verdict **avoid**: free base gone, weekly pricing creeping, cancellation dark patterns reported. A real company with liked bots, but ranked last here is no longer on brand with what we ship.

## Watchlist

- **Tipsy Chat** — scam-hunt caution (gem metering continues after payment; a $20-$54 billing bug reported); no fan-support ranking yet.
- **Candy AI** — scam-hunt caution: no breach, legit EU operator, but the flags are all monetization design (tokens on top of the sub).
- **Backyard AI** — active, but still cannot sell new subscriptions; unverifiable paid state.
- **Agnai (Agnaistic)** — light fan support (one dated recommendation write-up). Testable free, but needs more community weight to rank.
- **Muah.ai** — verdict **avoid** and confirmed here independently: the September 2024 breach (1.9M emails + prompts, HIBP/404 Media) plus community-reported astroturfing. It also survives only as a record, not a rank.
- **Rejected outright at v0** and standing: Anione (self-ranking blog), Replika (**avoid** — paid feature removed, no refunds, €5M Italy fine, AG petition trail), Character.AI (**avoid** — bankruptcy, 40+ state AGs), Moemate (defunct but still collecting affiliate money), Secret Desires AI (S3 exposure + no refunds).
