The AI companion category has a pricing problem. Almost every app advertises a free tier, almost every free tier is a preview, and the checkout page is often behind a login. This list is ordered by what a paying adult actually gets after the free trial ends: content freedom, memory, character variety, and whether the price survives a second month.

Ranking order is locked from verified evidence and never reads affiliate status. Every price table below pulls straight from its live entity dossier so this list stays in sync with our latest checks.

## How we ranked them

Each platform here was evaluated across five criteria—**memory and long-context quality, NSFW freedom, testability before paying, price transparency, and billing/privacy trust**—using two evidence layers:

- **First-party checks and sustained community feedback** — live pricing verification paired with dated discussions from active user communities (Reddit, Hacker News), never aggregator SEO lists.
- **Trust and billing audit** — documented red flags from 2023–2026 coverage (privacy profiles, billing/cancellation friction, security incidents, refund policies), rated clean, caution, or avoid.

**The rule:** only platforms with meaningful community support and no billing or security dealbreakers make the Top 10. Platforms rated **avoid** are dropped and named publicly below.

## The top 10

1. **[SillyTavern](/ai/sillytavern)** — *heavy community backing; no trust dealbreakers.* Free, open-source, zero platform censorship, and works with any local or API backend. Ranked first on NSFW freedom, privacy, and testability. Tradeoff: steep setup curve, and heavy external API use can add up quickly.
2. **[Janitor AI](/ai/janitor-ai)** — *heavy community backing; caution, no billing dealbreaker.* Huge free character catalog and uncensored text earn it #2 for breadth and free entry. Caution: back up your bots before paying, as users have reported a memory-wipe bug affecting long-context chats.
3. **[Kindroid](/ai/kindroid)** — *heavy community backing; caution.* Best-in-class docs and strong multimedia features. Caveats: a 2025 moderation shift narrowed its unfiltered stance, mobile store subscriptions cost more than web, and a Nov 2025 Play Store removal disrupted renewals.
4. **[Nomi](/ai/nomi)** — *heavy community backing; caution.* One of the cleanest flat-plan pricing structures in the category plus an indefinite free tier. Operational caveat: some users have reported personality resets after platform updates—stick to monthly billing until that settles.
5. **[Chub AI](/ai/chub-ai)** — *heavy community backing; caution.* Massive card library with bring-your-own-API flexibility. Flagged for beginners after a 2026 restructure cut the free tier and moved billing to crypto-only.
6. **[SpicyChat](/ai/spicychat)** — *moderate community backing; caution.* Large library, documented tier table, and `$5.95/mo` verified entry pricing. Caution: watch out for unofficial clone apps in mobile stores (use the web app) and mixed support/refund reports.
7. **[Dreamjourney AI](/ai/dreamjourney-ai)** — *moderate community backing; no red flags on file.* Its Memory Nexus system automates relationship pacing that rivals leave manual. Credit metering and desktop UI rough edges keep it outside the top five.
8. **[FictionLab](/ai/fictionlab)** — *moderate community backing; re-check flagged.* Scenario and world-building focus backed by in-house 70B+ models. Placed provisionally while we complete a direct pricing re-check past its Cloudflare gate.
9. **[WyvernChat](/ai/wyvernchat)** — *moderate community backing; no red flags on file.* Unfiltered, unlogged chats with free rotating models and scriptable bots. Interface complexity and no dedicated mobile app cap its position.
10. **[DreamGen](/ai/dreamgen)** — *cleanest trust record in the category.* Bootstrapped, honored refunds, and no documented security incidents. Smaller community footprint than the top picks, but its trust record earns it a spot on the board.

## Apps we dropped — and why

- **[CrushOn AI](/story/crushon-ai-review)** — *trust verdict: avoid.* Reported lack of self-serve cancellation, non-refundable terms, payment-processor churn, heavy third-party tracking (~45 trackers reported) with no confirmed encryption, and conflicting operator records. Its dossier stays live with a warning, and affiliate promotion is paused.
- **[CHAI](/ai/chai)** — *trust verdict: avoid.* Free baseline eroded, weekly pricing creep, and reported cancellation dark patterns.

## Watchlist

- **[Tipsy Chat](/ai/tipsy-chat)** — caution over post-payment gem metering and reported billing glitches.
- **[Candy AI](/ai/candy-ai)** — legitimate EU operator with no documented breach, held back by token-heavy monetization on top of subscriptions.
- **[Backyard AI](/ai/backyard-ai)** — active local tool, currently not selling new subscriptions.
- **Agnai (Agnaistic)** — testable free tier, still building broader community track record.
- **[Muah.ai](/ai/muah-ai)** — *verdict: avoid* following the September 2024 data breach (1.9M emails and prompts) and reported astroturfing.
- **Excluded:** Anione (self-ranking blog), Replika (paid features pulled, no refunds, €5M Italian regulatory fine), Character.AI (bankruptcy and state AG scrutiny), Moemate (defunct), and Secret Desires AI (S3 exposure and no-refund complaints).
