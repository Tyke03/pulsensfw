The AI companion category has a pricing problem. Almost every app advertises a free tier, almost every free tier is a preview, and the checkout page is often behind a login. This list is ordered by what a paying adult actually gets after the free trial ends: content freedom, memory, character variety, and whether the price survives a second month.

Two links on this page are tracked: CrushOn and SpicyChat. Both are marked in the entity record and both were ranked on the same criteria as the rest. Everything else is a plain link. The price tables live in the entity records so this page does not become a second, stale copy of the facts.

## Status: v0, under review

This is a first-pass editorial ranking, published so the weighting can be argued about in public. It is not a traffic survey and not a hands-on benchmark. Two candidates sit on the watchlist rather than the list:

- **Muah.ai** — came in through tracker sites only, and the desk's rule is that inclusion and rank cannot rest on aggregator lists. It keeps a catalog record until community evidence shows up.
- **Tipsy Chat** — first-party marketing is genuinely inviting (filter-free, ad-free, free use), but no price we can verify anywhere. It gets ranked when it gets priced.

## How the ranking works

In order: memory and long-context quality, NSFW freedom, the ability to test before paying (free tier or trial), price transparency and value, and community reputation among heavy users. Affiliate status was not a factor — it never is; tracked links are marked in the record, not the ranking.

A high score is not a safety badge. Read the privacy facts in each record before entering payment details, especially on apps distributed outside the major stores (SpicyChat ships as an Android APK, for instance).

The expectations check: Kindroid holds the top five on memory depth plus its unusually clear moderation docs. Chub, SpicyChat, CrushOn, Janitor AI, and Nomi all make the list as expected. DreamGen and Backyard AI were verified live before inclusion — DreamGen's pricing page and Backyard's plans page both answered as of 2026-10-27, though Backyard currently cannot sell new subscriptions at all.

## What to skip

If you only want a free roleplay session, start with the free tiers and do not subscribe on the first night. If you want explicit long-form chat with a consistent character, pay for memory rather than message volume. If you want images, budget the token meter separately; the subscription is rarely the whole bill — and note which apps ban explicit imagery outright (Nomi's no-nude rule, CHAI's explicit-media ban).
