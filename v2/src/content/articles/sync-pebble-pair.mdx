The pebbles themselves are the easy part. They sit where they should, they stay quiet, and the patterns are less cartoonish than the app icons suggest.

The link is the product. In this seed session it died every time the phone locked, which is what phones do. A couples toy that requires the screen to stay awake is a toy with a chaperone.

## Versus the wand
If you want one device that just runs, the wand is the calmer buy. If you want two devices that talk, wait for a link that does not panic.
